THE BASICS

What is AI impersonation?

Scammers can use artificial intelligence to create or change messages, images, audio, and video to pretend to be someone else. They may use these tools to make a request for money or personal information seem more believable.

AI content can be difficult to recognize, so check the request itself instead of relying only on how something looks or sounds.

KNOW WHAT TO LOOK FOR

Common warning signs

Pressure to act fast

Someone says there is an emergency and you need to send money immediately.

A request for secrecy

You are told not to speak to anyone else about the situation.

An unusual request

A person asks for money, passwords, or sensitive information in an unexpected way.

Example: an unexpected emergency

A voice message sounds like your friend and says, “I need money right now. Please don’t tell anyone.” Even if the voice sounds familiar, pause and contact your friend using a number you already know.

TAKE A MOMENT

How to verify a request

01

Pause

Don’t let urgency push you into sending money, sharing information, or clicking a link.

02

Contact them directly

Call or message the person using a number or account you already know. Don’t rely on contact details in the suspicious message.

03

Check with someone you trust

If you can’t reach the person, ask a trusted friend or family member to help confirm the story.

If you already sent money, contact the payment provider as soon as possible and ask about recovering it. You can report fraud at ReportFraud.ftc.gov.
